## Interpretation of Factor f1 (negative pole)

Although the edition means are all positive, the **negative pole of f1 is represented relatively by the lowest-scoring editions**, especially **vem_ed_12** Mean 2.25, **vem_ed_07** Mean 4.25, and **vem_ed_04** Mean 6.25. The example texts are also concentrated in these three editions, so they appear to be the main drivers of this pole.

This pole encodes a discourse of **institutionalised virtual international collaboration**, especially around **PCI/COIL projects**, their organisation, evaluation, dissemination, and pedagogical practice. It is strongly associated with the newsletter’s reporting of partnerships between Fatecs/Cesu/Centro Paula Souza and foreign institutions, with emphasis on project management, intercultural exchange, academic events, applied professional themes, and evidence of results.

### Main proposed label

**“Institutional reporting on PCI/COIL internationalisation practices”**

This is the strongest label because it captures both the lexical loadings and the examples. The pole is not simply about “internationalisation” in the abstract; it is about internationalisation as an organised institutional programme: projects are planned, scheduled, evaluated, publicised, continued, revised, and reported through newsletter-style accounts.

---

## Justification from the factor loadings

The loading words point to several overlapping discourse clusters.

### 1. Programme administration, planning and evaluation

Many high-loading items relate to the management and assessment of institutional projects:

- **administrar, agendar, agenda, alteração, revisão, informar, relatório, questionário, pesquisado, levantamento, mapeamento, desempenho, requisito, letivo, semestral, disponibilidade, procedimento, meta, operacional**

These words suggest a discourse of **project coordination and quality control**. The concern is not only that collaborations happen, but that they are monitored, evaluated, adjusted and incorporated into institutional routines.

This is directly reflected in the vem_ed_12 examples, especially the texts on PCI perception surveys:

> “As pesquisas de percepção, realizadas desde 2020 para professores e estudantes envolvidos nos PCIs das Fatecs, são aplicadas no fim de cada semestre letivo.”

Loading words present here include **administrar, resumos, letivo, desempenho, chance**, and in the following example **pesquisado, concordar, pretender, questionário, agosto**. This confirms that the pole includes an evaluative-administrative discourse around measuring participation, satisfaction, continuity and perceived academic/professional benefits.

### 2. Academic collaboration and event discourse

The loadings include numerous terms connected with academic meetings, presentations, publication and higher-education structures:

- **keynote, speaker, conferencista, seminário, colóquio, moderador, palestraram, resumos, submissão, aceito, coautoria, mestrado, doutor, pós-graduando, campus, campi, reitor, comitê**

These terms indicate a discourse of **formal academic exchange**: seminars, colloquia, webinars, presentations, calls, summaries, submissions and institutional actors.

This is particularly visible in vem_ed_07 and vem_ed_04. For example, the Uniminuto collaboration includes the **Colóquio de Literatura Brasileira**, with named speakers and institutional representatives. The Chile/Inacap text in vem_ed_04 reports a series of webinars, with loading words such as **manufatura, mineração, moderar, campus, palestraram, operação, indústria, operacional, fundador**.

Thus, the pole includes the newsletter genre of **documenting academic events and partnership activities**.

### 3. Intercultural and multilingual collaboration

The loadings also contain many words signalling international, multilingual and cross-cultural contexts:

- **latinoamericano, latino-americano, luso-brasileiro, norte-americana, holandês, chinês, indiana, emigrar, refugiado**
- English/Spanish or hybrid educational terms: **keynote, speaker, pitch, icebreaker, approach, perspective, proyecto, aprendizaje, desarrollo, rompehielo**
- language/culture terms: **translinguagem, sotaque, idiomático, jargão, semiótico, gíria**

This lexical profile strongly matches the corpus context: VEm reports on virtual exchanges between Brazilian Fatecs and institutions in Portugal, Colombia, Chile, the United States and elsewhere. The pole is especially linked to **intercultural pedagogical collaboration**, not merely international news.

The examples reinforce this. Vem_ed_07 foregrounds Brazil-Colombia collaborations, Spanish/Portuguese interaction, Uniminuto partnerships, and topics such as culture, language, publicity and literature. Vem_ed_12 foregrounds Brazil-Portugal collaborations with the University of Aveiro, including terms such as **equipa**, **pitch**, **turismo**, **guia**, **destino turístico**.

### 4. Practical pedagogy and “best practices” for virtual exchange

Another important lexical cluster concerns tools, classroom practices and instructional design:

- **slide, arquivo, rubrica, guia, treinamento, coach, conduzer, guiar, ementa, híbrido, videoconferência, link, acessibilidade, imersão, icebreaker**

This points to a discourse of **how to run virtual international projects effectively**. The pole includes practical advice for teachers: planning activities, structuring interaction, using digital tools, creating rubrics, preparing students and managing synchronous/asynchronous communication.

This is very clear in f1_neg_005, “Incrementar a interação”, where the teacher describes planning, icebreakers, narrated PowerPoint slides, MP4 files, synchronous meetings, rubrics and assessment. The loading words in this excerpt — **agendar, slide, rubrica, arquivo, icebreaker, aberto, compartilha, equipamento** — fit this pedagogical-practical cluster very closely.

### 5. Applied professional and technological domains

The loadings also contain many terms from specific professional or technical fields:

- **manufatura, mineração, indústria, operação, operacional**
- **turismo, turístico, hotel, destino, gastronomia**
- **publicidade, publicitário, mídia**
- **médico-hospitalar, equipamento, física, química**
- **logística** appears in the examples, though not in the loading list shown.

This indicates that the pole is connected to the **applied-disciplinary content** of PCI/COIL projects. The discourse is not only institutional; it reports the subject matter of collaborations: tourism, marketing, logistics, mining, manufacturing, medical equipment, physics, literature, publicity and semiotics.

The examples from vem_ed_04 and vem_ed_07 strongly illustrate this. The Inacap collaboration involves logistics, drones, mining, manufacturing and agribusiness. The BridgeValley collaboration involves medical-hospital equipment and physics of waves. The University of Aveiro collaboration includes tourism guides and marketing.

### 6. Positive institutional affect and achievement discourse

A further cluster contains evaluative and celebratory terms:

- **orgulhar, brindar, fantástico, louvar, elogiar, dedicação, persistência, determinação, resiliêncio, inspirar, entusiasmar, comemorar, ganho, chance**

This suggests that the pole also includes the newsletter’s promotional/institutional voice: it celebrates achievements, recognises effort, and frames PCIs as successful, inspiring and beneficial.

This is especially visible in vem_ed_04:

> “A Coordenação de Línguas se orgulha das realizações neste 2020 desafiador. A equipe dos Projetos Colaborativos Internacionais demonstrou resiliência e determinação.”

The loading words **orgulhar, dedicação, comemorar, determinação** occur in this excerpt, confirming the celebratory institutional reporting function of the pole.

## Justification from the examples

The examples show three main realisations of the pole.

### vem_ed_12: evaluation and consolidation of PCI practices

Vem_ed_12 is the lowest-scoring edition and therefore the strongest edition-level indicator for this pole. Its examples focus on:

- collaborations with the University of Aveiro;
- continuity of projects across semesters;
- tourism, marketing and communication projects;
- surveys of student and teacher perceptions;
- quantification of participation and outcomes;
- intentions to maintain or change projects and partners.

The discourse here is highly administrative and evaluative. It presents PCI/COIL as an established institutional programme that can be measured, improved and expanded.

Representative loading words in the examples include:

- **hotel, alteração, dupla, pitch, colaborações, equipa, prosseguir, turístico, turismo, guia, destino**
- **administrar, resumos, letivo, desempenho, chance**
- **pesquisado, concordar, pretender, questionário, agosto**

This strongly supports the label **institutional reporting on PCI/COIL internationalisation practices**, especially in its evaluation-and-continuity aspect.

### vem_ed_07: Latin American partnerships, intercultural projects and best practices

Vem_ed_07 is the second-lowest edition mean and is heavily represented among the examples. It foregrounds:

- diversification of partnerships with Latin American institutions;
- Uniminuto/Colombia collaborations;
- culture, language, advertising, semiotics and literature;
- the Colóquio de Literatura Brasileira;
- BridgeValley partnerships on medical equipment and physics;
- teacher guidance on increasing interaction in PCIs.

The pole here is realised as **intercultural collaborative pedagogy**, with strong attention to project themes, institutional partners, and practical teaching strategies.

Representative loading words include:

- **latinoamericano, publicidade, colóquio, médico-hospitalar, equipamento**
- **agendar, slide, rubrica, arquivo, icebreaker**
- **assertividade, semiótico, jargão, publicitário, influência**
- **moderar, requisito, física, mulher**

This edition broadens the pole from administrative evaluation to include **cultural, linguistic and pedagogical exchange**.

### vem_ed_04: early institutionalisation, webinars and COIL origin narratives

Vem_ed_04 also scores low and contributes examples focused on:

- early pandemic-era expansion of PCIs;
- institutional achievement narratives;
- Chile/Inacap collaborations;
- webinars on logistics, mining, manufacturing and drones;
- Jon Rubin’s account of the origins and development of COIL.

Representative loading words include:

- **manufatura, mineração, moderar, campus, palestraram, operação, indústria, operacional, fundador**
- **orgulhar, dedicação, comemorar, determinação**
- **mídia, filme, persistência, inspirar, fundador, lançar, imersão, entusiasmar**

This edition gives the pole a historical and legitimising dimension: it situates PCIs/COILs as an innovative educational model requiring persistence, institutional support, partnership-building and adaptation to virtual exchange.

## Discourses encoded at this pole

Overall, f1 negative encodes a discourse dimension centred on:

1. **PCI/COIL programme institutionalisation**  
   Reporting virtual international projects as organised, recurring, assessable institutional activities.

2. **International academic partnership-building**  
   Naming partner institutions, countries, coordinators, professors, students and collaborative outputs.

3. **Pedagogical practice and project management**  
   Emphasising planning, icebreakers, tools, rubrics, interaction, scheduling, communication and evaluation.

4. **Applied interdisciplinary collaboration**  
   Presenting projects through concrete subject areas such as tourism, logistics, mining, manufacturing, medical equipment, physics, publicity, literature and marketing.

5. **Intercultural and multilingual exchange**  
   Highlighting language, culture, Latin American collaboration, Portuguese/Spanish/English terminology, and cross-cultural learning.

6. **Institutional achievement and improvement**  
   Celebrating growth, resilience, dedication, student participation, teacher engagement and survey-based evidence of success.

## Possible labels for f1 negative

### 1. **Institutional reporting on PCI/COIL internationalisation practices**

This is the best overall label. It captures the programme-centred nature of the pole, the newsletter reporting genre, and the recurring focus on virtual international collaboration, assessment, planning and continuity.

### 2. **Managed virtual exchange and collaborative pedagogy**

This label foregrounds the pedagogical and operational aspects: icebreakers, rubrics, scheduling, interaction, project design, teacher guidance and student participation. It fits especially well with vem_ed_07 and vem_ed_12.

### 3. **Programme evaluation and dissemination of international academic partnerships**

This label is useful if one wants to emphasise the survey, reporting and institutional communication side of the pole. It fits the vem_ed_12 perception survey texts and the many loading words related to questionnaires, summaries, performance, reports, seminars and public events.

### 4. **Institutionalised intercultural project work in applied higher education**

This label captures the applied-disciplinary content of the pole: tourism, logistics, mining, manufacturing, publicity, medical equipment, physics and marketing. It also reflects the Fatec context, where internationalisation is linked to professional and technological education.

### 5. **Celebratory documentation of PCI/COIL growth and partnerships**

This label highlights the promotional newsletter voice: pride, celebration, resilience, dedication, successful expansion, new partners and continued projects. It is particularly relevant to vem_ed_04.

---

## Recommended label

The most comprehensive label is:

**Institutional reporting on PCI/COIL internationalisation practices**

A fuller descriptive version would be:

**Institutional reporting on the planning, evaluation and dissemination of PCI/COIL virtual international collaborations in applied higher education.**

This wording accommodates both the lexical loadings and the examples: it includes administration, surveys, academic events, intercultural partnerships, pedagogical tools, applied project themes and the institutional celebration of programme growth.
