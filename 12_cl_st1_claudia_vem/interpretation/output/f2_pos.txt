## Interpretation of Factor f2 positive pole

Factor f2 positive appears to encode a discourse of **institutionalised virtual exchange/COIL as a managed, sustainable, research-informed educational innovation**, often illustrated through **PCI/COIL project management, staff training, international partnership-building, dissemination at events, and sustainability/ODS-themed projects**.

The pole is very strongly driven by **vem_ed_19**, whose mean score is an extreme outlier: **30.0**, far above the next highest editions such as **vem_ed_31** 8.0, **vem_ed_27** 7.5, **vem_ed_26** 7.4, and **vem_ed_08** 7.2. All supplied high-scoring excerpts come from **vem_ed_19**, so this edition is central to the interpretation. The examples show a newsletter issue focused on PCIs/COIL, sustainability projects, interviews with virtual-exchange leaders, and reports from international COIL events.

### Preferred label

**“Institutionalising and sustaining COIL/virtual exchange through project management, training, and international collaboration”**

This label best captures the convergence of the loading vocabulary and the excerpts.

The factor loadings include many terms associated with **programme design and management**: *gerenciar, design, implantação, institucionalizar, escopo, critério, planejado, definir, passo, intervenção, emergência, continuidade, qualidade, suporte, treinamento, instrucional, assíncrono, on-line, matchmaking*. These words point to a discourse concerned not simply with doing international virtual projects, but with **organising, scaling, supporting, monitoring, and improving them institutionally**.

The examples strongly confirm this. Excerpts from the interview with Rosi León discuss how DePaul implemented GLE/COIL through *treinamento on-line*, *design instrucional*, *atividades assíncronas*, *suporte tecnológico*, partner-finding or *matchmaking*, and preparation modules for students. Other excerpts report conference panels on COIL management and explicitly list steps for coordinator intervention: *institucionalizar*, *definir claramente o papel do coordenador*, *alinhar expectativas*, *determinar critérios*, *gerenciar o design dos projetos*, and *decidir quando intervir*. This is a managerial and institutional discourse of virtual exchange.

### Supporting discourse components

#### 1. Sustainability as both theme and programme principle

A prominent secondary discourse is **sustainability**, in two senses.

First, the loadings include environmental and energy vocabulary: *climático, renovável, reciclagem, eficiência, energia, planeta, sustentabilidade, ambiental, sustentável, alternativa*. In the excerpts, this is visible in the Fatec Franco da Rocha PCIs on **Objetivos de Desenvolvimento Sustentável**, **energias renováveis**, **fornos solares**, **mudanças climáticas**, and “pensar globalmente / agir localmente”.

Second, sustainability also refers to the **durability and continuity of COIL programmes**. The examples speak of maintaining virtual exchanges “por um longo tempo”, repeating projects across semesters, using the same project structure with different student groups, building stable institutional support, and expanding COIL “com qualidade”. Loading words such as *continuidade, manter, estável, duração, oferta, expansão, qualidade* support this programme-sustainability reading.

A possible sub-label would therefore be:

**“Sustainable COIL: environmental themes and long-term programme continuity”**

This is not separate from the main interpretation; rather, it is one of the major semantic strands within the pole.

#### 2. Professional-academic dissemination of COIL expertise

The pole also contains a discourse of **knowledge-sharing, research, and professional dissemination**. The loadings include *congresso, webinário, painel, entrevista, newsletter, matéria, periódico, capítulo, livro, publicar, investigação, multidisciplinar, campo, pós-graduação*. In the examples, high-scoring texts report presentations at the **Red LatAM COIL Congress** and **COIL Connect**, discuss publishing articles and chapters on virtual exchange, and present interviews with experienced COIL practitioners.

This gives the pole a metadiscursive quality: it is not only about classroom projects, but about **COIL as an object of professional reflection, academic research, and international community-building**.

A possible label foregrounding this aspect would be:

**“Professional dissemination and research discourse around COIL/virtual exchange”**

#### 3. Interdisciplinary and interinstitutional collaboration

The loadings also point to interdisciplinarity and institutional partnership: *interinstitucional, multidisciplinar, interdisciplinar, transversal, sinergia, parceria, coordenadora, direção, gestor, campo, disciplinas, inglês, tradução, gramática*. The examples repeatedly describe collaboration among technical-discipline teachers, language teachers, coordinators, directors, international partners, and support offices. The projects combine management, English, energy, sustainability, negotiation, and intercultural learning.

This suggests a discourse of **cross-disciplinary, cross-institutional coordination**, where successful internationalisation depends on aligning multiple actors and knowledge domains.

A possible label here would be:

**“Interinstitutional and interdisciplinary coordination of virtual-exchange projects”**

### Role of vem_ed_19

The positive pole is overwhelmingly exemplified by **vem_ed_19**. This edition appears to be a special or highly concentrated issue on PCIs/COIL, with three linked emphases:

1. **Sustainability-themed PCIs** at Fatec Franco da Rocha, especially ODS, renewable energy, energy efficiency, and climate change.
2. **Interview-based expertise** on implementing and scaling virtual exchange, especially the DePaul GLE/COIL experience.
3. **International COIL professional events**, including congresses, webinars, panels, coordinator intervention, quality assurance, and programme expansion.

Because vem_ed_19 has such a high mean and all examples come from it, the factor is strongly shaped by this edition’s particular combination of **COIL institutionalisation + sustainability + professional dissemination**. Other relatively high-scoring editions may share some of this vocabulary, but the evidence provided makes vem_ed_19 the clearest driver.

## Suggested labels for f2 positive

1. **Institutionalising and sustaining COIL/virtual exchange through project management, training, and international collaboration**  
   *Best overall label; captures management, support, continuity, online design, and partnership-building.*

2. **Sustainable COIL and virtual-exchange programme development**  
   *Highlights the double meaning of sustainability: environmental/ODS themes and long-term maintenance of exchange programmes.*

3. **Professional and academic discourse on COIL implementation, research, and dissemination**  
   *Foregrounds conferences, webinars, interviews, publications, research, and expert knowledge-sharing.*

4. **Interdisciplinary, interinstitutional management of international online learning projects**  
   *Emphasises the coordination of teachers, managers, disciplines, languages, technologies, and partner institutions.*

Overall, the most adequate interpretation is that f2 positive represents a discourse of **managed, institutionally supported, sustainability-oriented COIL/PCI practice**, presented through newsletter reporting, expert interviews, project testimonials, and international professional dissemination.
