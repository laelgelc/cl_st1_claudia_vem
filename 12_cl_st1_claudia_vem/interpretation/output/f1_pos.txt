## Interpretation of f1 (positive)

**Recommended label: Academic dissemination and institutional development of inclusive internationalisation**

This pole encodes discourse that **reports, discusses and legitimises virtual exchange as an institutional and pedagogical approach to internationalisation**. Academic events and research provide the settings in which practices are shared, evaluated and connected to inclusion, intercultural belonging and critical learning.

The interpretation combines two closely connected strands:

1. **Academic-professional dissemination:** coverage of conferences, panels, thematic discussions, research and project presentations.
2. **Institutional and socially oriented internationalisation:** embedding virtual exchange in curricula and institutional policies while addressing access, linguistic mediation, identity and critical engagement.

Neither strand alone adequately captures the complete loading pattern.

### 1. Evidence from the lexical loadings

The twelve loading words form several overlapping semantic clusters:

| Lexical cluster | Loading words | Discourse contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Academic reporting and knowledge circulation | **cobertura** (1.03), **painel** (.89), **temático** (.80), **descrever** (1.08) | Reporting events, organising discussion and describing practices or findings |
| Research and institutional embedding | **tese** (1.01), **institucionalização** (.95), **eixo** (.76) | Connecting projects to scholarly inquiry, institutional structures and pedagogical organising principles |
| Inclusion and intercultural identification | **pertencimento** (1.08), **inclusão** (.84) | Presenting internationalisation as access to participation and recognition of cultural identities |
| Linguistic and critical mediation | **recepção** (.96), **tradução** (.84), **crítico** (.77) | Interpreting cultural productions, mediating languages and developing critical engagement |

These groupings are contextual rather than fixed dictionary categories. For example, **eixo** denotes both an administrative area and a pedagogical guiding principle in the examples. **Tradução** includes practical language mediation but also editorial translation of presentation titles. **Crítico** covers critical reception, literacy, thinking and examination of theory and practice.

The full loading set therefore supports a broader interpretation than simply “conference reporting” or “decolonial education”: it links **knowledge circulation, institutional organisation and socially attentive pedagogy**.

### 2. Evidence from the high-scoring excerpts

The most concentrated illustration is **t000143, edition 27, score 12**, which contains all twelve loading words. Its coverage of the Jornada de PCIs brings together:

- **cobertura**, **painéis temáticos** and descriptions of presentations;
- research represented by a doctoral **tese**;
- explicit **institucionalização** of Internationalisation at Home;
- **inclusão** through overcoming financial and linguistic barriers;
- identity and **pertencimento**;
- **recepção crítica** of audiovisual productions;
- practical **traduções** in a nursing collaboration.

Crucially, these words occur across different sections of a multi-presentation report. Their concentration reflects the text’s breadth as well as the thematic connections among the presentations; it should not be treated as evidence that every word belongs to one narrowly defined discourse.

The other excerpts establish that the interpretation is not dependent solely on this report:

- **Edition 25** repeatedly combines event organisation with inclusion. The RedAES report links **mesas temáticas** to accessibility and implementation challenges; the Red LatAM COIL report combines **painel** and **inclusão** with community building and socially relevant project design.
- **Edition 27’s opinion article** connects **tese** and **pertencimento** to research-informed reflection on identity, reciprocal learning and decolonial internationalisation.
- **Edition 29** links **crítico** and **inclusão** to equitable partnerships, accessible technologies, critical literacy and socially engaged projects. Its symposium report also discusses the relationship between research and practice.
- **Edition 31** extends this discourse to responsible internationalisation, ethical global engagement, critical thinking and research-based evaluation of learning.

Decoloniality is thus an important illustrated strand, but **not a sufficient label for the entire pole**. Several examples concern institutional support, socioemotional competencies and professional preparation without centring decolonial critique.

### Possible labels

1. **Academic dissemination and institutional development of inclusive internationalisation** — **preferred**, because it accommodates the complete loading pattern and the range of examples.
2. **Research-informed, inclusive virtual-exchange discourse** — foregrounds the relationship between scholarship, project practice and equitable participation, but makes event coverage less explicit.
3. **Academic-event reporting and critical intercultural pedagogy** — captures the prominent reporting format and pedagogical concerns, though it underrepresents institutionalisation.

Overall, f1 positive is best understood as **the academic and institutional articulation of virtual exchange, circulated through events and research and framed by inclusive, intercultural and critical educational aims**.
