## Factor f5 (positive): interpretation as a discourse dimension

The positive pole of Factor 5 appears to encode a discourse of **virtual internationalisation through COIL/PCI as a transformative, human-centred, institutionally maturing practice**. It is strongly associated with texts that discuss **Intercâmbios Virtuais / Projetos Colaborativos Internacionais**, their expansion, challenges, pedagogical value, institutionalisation, equity concerns, and their role in building global, intercultural and professional competencies.

This pole is driven most clearly by **vem_ed_34**, whose mean score is exceptionally high (**22.8**) and whose highest-scoring text, t000182, has an extremely high factor score. **vem_ed_35** also scores strongly (**8.0**) and continues the same thematic pattern, especially around IVES 2026, COIL cooperation, obstacles, bridges, and boas práticas. Other high-scoring editions include **vem_ed_24, vem_ed_25, vem_ed_32, vem_ed_30, vem_ed_27, vem_ed_18, vem_ed_29, and vem_ed_31**, suggesting that this discourse is not isolated to one issue, although editions 34 and 35 clearly intensify and exemplify it.

## Main proposed label

### **“Transformative COIL/Virtual Exchange and Sustainable Internationalisation at Home”**

This is the strongest label because it captures the main semantic fields in both the loadings and the examples:

- **COIL / Virtual Exchange / PCIs / Internacionalização em Casa**
- **transformative learning and intercultural development**
- **institutional consolidation, sustainability and scaling**
- **human connection, collaboration and trust**
- **equity, inclusion, justice and decolonial awareness**
- **obstacles of online international collaboration and strategies to overcome them**

The label should not be understood as referring only to technology-mediated exchange. The pole is more specifically about **the discourse that frames virtual exchange as a strategic, ethical, pedagogical and institutional project**.

## Justification from the factor loadings

The lexical loadings point to several tightly connected discourse strands.

### 1. Virtual international collaboration and COIL ecology

Many loadings directly evoke the field of international virtual exchange:

- **telecolaboração**
- **intercâmbios / intercâmbio**
- **international**
- **universidade**
- **educador**
- **currículo**
- **rede**
- **consórcio**
- **ecossistema**
- **glocal**
- **continente**
- **fronteira**
- **lusófono**
- **exchange**

These words suggest a discourse centred on **international academic networks**, **cross-border educational practices**, and **collaborative online learning ecosystems**. The presence of terms such as *telecolaboração*, *intercâmbios*, *rede*, *consórcio* and *ecossistema* indicates that the pole is not merely about individual exchange experiences, but about a broader organised field of practice.

### 2. Transformation, development and professional formation

A second strong lexical cluster concerns educational transformation and student development:

- **transformador**
- **transformar**
- **formar**
- **carreira**
- **aprendizado**
- **pensamento**
- **crítico**
- **relevância**
- **potência**
- **impacto**
- **competências** are not in the loading list as such, but are strongly represented in the examples.

This vocabulary frames COIL/PCI not as an auxiliary activity, but as a **transformative pedagogical practice** that develops intercultural, socioemotional, linguistic, professional and global competencies.

### 3. Human-centred collaboration: empathy, trust, solidarity

A very important part of the pole is the human and ethical vocabulary:

- **solidariedade**
- **justiça**
- **equidade**
- **empatia / empatir**
- **emocional**
- **confiança**
- **compromisso**
- **comunitário**
- **interdependência**
- **paixão**
- **reconhecimento**

These loadings indicate that the discourse is not only administrative or technical. It presents international virtual collaboration as a **human relational practice**, built through trust, empathy, commitment, solidarity and mutual recognition.

### 4. Challenges, barriers and complexity of virtual exchange

Another highly salient lexical group concerns obstacles and constraints:

- **dificultar**
- **obstáculo**
- **limitação**
- **limitar**
- **isolamento**
- **adversidade**
- **complexidade**
- **fuso**
- **celular**
- **tela**
- **domínio**
- **condição**
- **depender**
- **exigir**
- **estrutural**

These words show that high-scoring texts do not simply celebrate internationalisation. They also discuss the practical, linguistic, technological, temporal and institutional difficulties involved in virtual exchange: time zones, connectivity, mobile-phone access, language barriers, limited institutional support, workload, uncertainty and structural inequalities.

### 5. Institutionalisation, sustainability and scaling

A further cluster points to consolidation and long-term development:

- **duradouro**
- **sólido**
- **escala**
- **expansão / expandir**
- **modelo**
- **marco**
- **articulação**
- **institucional**
- **suporte**
- **reconhecimento**
- **fundação**
- **consórcio**
- **rede**

This suggests a discourse of **maturing practice**: COIL/PCI is represented as moving from individual enthusiasm or isolated projects toward scalable, recognisable, institutionally supported internationalisation.

## Justification from the example excerpts

The examples strongly support this interpretation.

### vem_ed_34: the main driver of the pole

The most important example is **t000182 from vem_ed_34**, with a score of **88**, far higher than the other excerpts. It is a long report on a webinar celebrating 20 years of COIL, with speakers from Africa, Europe, Latin America and the United States. It contains a very dense concentration of loading words, including:

- **telecolaboração**
- **ecossistema**
- **glocal**
- **interdependência**
- **equidade**
- **solidariedade**
- **decolonização**
- **justiça**
- **transformador**
- **obstáculo**
- **limitação**
- **fuso**
- **celular**
- **currículo**
- **rede**
- **consórcio**
- **universidade**
- **intercâmbios**

This text almost functions as a prototype of the positive pole. It frames COIL as a global movement that has expanded over two decades, but also as a practice facing structural challenges: linguistic inequality, lack of institutional support, mobility privilege, regional asymmetries, technical limitations, and uncertainty about how to evaluate and recognise virtual exchange.

The discourse is simultaneously:

- celebratory: COIL as a transformative global educational practice;
- strategic: how to scale, institutionalise and measure impact;
- ethical: justice, equity, solidarity, decoloniality;
- pedagogical: intercultural communication, empathy, collaboration, critical thinking;
- practical: time zones, screens, mobile phones, calendars, language barriers.

This combination is central to the factor.

### Boas práticas: concrete COIL/PCI experiences

Excerpts **t000184**, **t000189**, and **t000188** show how the same discourse appears in practical project reports.

In **t000184**, the PCI between Fatec Americana and CEAM Peru is narrated as an intercultural, professional and disciplinary experience in fashion and textile education. Loading words such as **sólido**, **transformar**, **excelente**, and **comentar** appear in a context where students build a “relação sólida” and learn beyond the conventional classroom.

In **t000189**, from **vem_ed_35**, the PCI with Setsunan University in Japan centres on collaboration across time zones, cultures and technological constraints. Loading words include:

- **celular**
- **escala**
- **compromisso**
- **obstáculo**
- **transformar**
- **confiança**
- **duradouro**
- **fuso**
- **international**

This example shows the pole’s concern with the practical mechanics of virtual collaboration: asynchronous tasks, Padlet, videos, mobile phones, teacher workload, engagement and the 12-hour time difference. Yet these challenges are framed positively as material for intercultural learning and durable connection.

In **t000188**, the PCI with Chile around coffee and chocolate gives a more affective version of the same dimension. Loading words such as **emocional**, **confiança**, **reconhecimento**, **fronteira**, **falar**, **transformar**, and **international** appear in a narrative of memory, identity, culture and human connection. This supports the interpretation that the pole includes a strong **humanistic and affective discourse of internationalisation**, not just administrative reporting.

## Possible labels for this pole

### 1. **“Transformative COIL/Virtual Exchange and Sustainable Internationalisation at Home”**

Best overall label. It captures the dominant focus on COIL, PCIs, virtual exchange, internationalisation at home, transformation, sustainability and institutional consolidation.

### 2. **“Human-Centred Virtual International Collaboration”**

This foregrounds the strong ethical and relational vocabulary: **empatia, confiança, solidariedade, compromisso, interdependência, comunitário, emocional**. It is suitable if one wants to emphasise that the pole is less about technology itself and more about human connection mediated by technology.

### 3. **“Institutionalising Global Collaborative Learning”**

This label highlights the discourse of scaling, recognition, networks, curricula, consortia and durable partnerships: **escala, rede, consórcio, currículo, institucional, reconhecimento, duradouro, sólido**. It is especially appropriate for the texts on FAUBAI, IVES, COIL Connect, BRaVE and PCI sustainability.

### 4. **“Equitable and Glocal Internationalisation through PCIs”**

This label captures the social justice and decolonial strand: **equidade, justiça, solidariedade, decolonização, glocal, lusófono, fronteira, comunidade**. It reflects the way the examples discuss access to international experiences for students who cannot travel, linguistic inequalities, and the need for inclusive internationalisation.

### 5. **“Obstacles and Bridges in COIL Cooperation”**

This label is closely aligned with **vem_ed_35**, especially the IVES 2026 theme. It captures the recurring vocabulary of **obstáculo, limitação, complexidade, fuso, celular, confiança, compromisso, transformar**. It is slightly narrower than the main label, because it foregrounds the challenge-management discourse within the broader COIL/PCI dimension.

## Recommended final label

The most comprehensive label is:

### **“Transformative COIL/Virtual Exchange and Sustainable Internationalisation at Home”**

This pole represents VEm discourse in which PCIs/COIL are framed as a mature and expanding educational practice: globally networked, pedagogically transformative, ethically committed to equity and inclusion, dependent on human relationships and institutional support, and shaped by the practical complexities of online intercultural collaboration.
