## Factor f6 (positive): discourse interpretation

The positive pole of Factor 6 appears to encode a discourse of **institutionalised virtual internationalisation in higher education**, especially through **PCIs/COIL/Virtual Exchange events, project reports, conference summaries, pedagogical benefits, and applied intercultural collaboration**.

This pole is strongly driven by **VEm edition 29** and **VEm edition 32**, which have by far the highest mean scores:

- **vem_ed_29: Mean f6 = 12.50**
- **vem_ed_32: Mean f6 = 9.29**

The high-scoring excerpts are almost entirely from these two editions. Edition 29 centres on **IVES Cesu 2025**, Faubai 2025, and opinion pieces on cultural exchange in PCIs. Edition 32 centres on the **Jornada dos PCIs 2025**, IVEC 2025, international visitors, UNICollaboration, and COIL/PCI benefit discussions. These editions therefore seem to drive the pole through concentrated coverage of **virtual exchange as an organised, institutional, pedagogical and internationalisation practice**.

## Main discourse encoded at this pole

### 1. Organised discourse of Virtual Exchange / COIL / PCI internationalisation

The clearest discourse is that of **Virtual Exchange and COIL as an institutional strategy for internationalisation of higher education**. The examples repeatedly mention:

- “Projetos Colaborativos Internacionais”
- “Intercâmbios Virtuais”
- “COIL”
- “International Virtual Exchange Symposium”
- “Internationalização do Currículo”
- “Internacionalização em Casa”
- “Global Learning”
- “Jornada dos PCIs”
- “IVEC”
- “Faubai”

This is strongly supported by the factor loadings. Many loading words belong to an event-based, institutional and academic internationalisation register:

- **exchange**
- **multicultural**
- **trilíngue**
- **currículo**
- **interdisciplinar**
- **transversal**
- **instituições**
- **faculdade**
- **licenciatura**
- **unidade**
- **gabinete**
- **coordenadora**
- **responsável**
- **congresso**
- **webinário**
- **workshop**
- **palestrante**
- **programação**
- **apresentação**
- **gravação**
- **canal**
- **podcast**

The examples show these lexical items functioning in a discourse of organised academic internationalisation. For instance, excerpt f6_pos_001 reports IVES Cesu 2025, names institutional actors, gives links to recordings on YouTube, summarises presentations, and frames COIL/Virtual Exchange as a way to develop global learning and intercultural collaboration. Excerpt f6_pos_005 similarly reports the Jornada dos PCIs 2025, with programming, speakers, definitions, statistics, recordings, project presentations and outcomes.

This pole is therefore not simply about “international topics” in general, but about **formalised internationalisation activities mediated through events, projects, institutional coordination and pedagogical frameworks**.

### 2. Event-reporting and conference-summary discourse

A second strong component is the **newsletter/reporting style used to summarise academic events**. The pole contains many words associated with conferences, webinars and organised programmes:

- **palestrante**
- **palestra magistral**
- **programação**
- **apresentação**
- **gravação**
- **canal**
- **webinário**
- **workshop**
- **congresso**
- **mesa-redonda**
- **relatar**
- **resumir**
- **comentar**
- **destacar**
- **debater**

The high-scoring excerpts are structured as institutional reports: they announce an event, give dates, list speakers, describe sessions, summarise content and often provide links to recordings or playlists. For example:

- f6_pos_001 reports the IVES Cesu 2025 symposium, gives participant numbers, lists speakers and summarises each presentation.
- f6_pos_005 reports the Jornada dos PCIs 2025, including registration numbers, satisfaction survey results, YouTube recordings, lecture summaries and project presentations.
- f6_pos_008 reports visits by international academics and their presentations.
- f6_pos_009 reports a UNICollaboration webinar.
- f6_pos_168 reports participation in IVEC 2025 and mentions workshops and thematic meetings.

The loading word **apresentação** appears across many examples, and **palestrante**, **programação**, **webinário**, **workshop**, **gravação** and **canal** reinforce the interpretation of this pole as a discourse of **event documentation and knowledge dissemination** within the VEm newsletter.

### 3. Pedagogical-benefit discourse: soft skills, interculturality and global learning

A further central strand is the discourse of **educational benefits**, especially the development of soft skills, intercultural competence, socioemotional learning, employability and student engagement.

The loadings include:

- **skills**
- **soft**
- **empatia**
- **resiliência**
- **bem-estar**
- **socioemocional**
- **motivação**
- **engajar**
- **benefício**
- **melhorar**
- **melhoria**
- **aprimor**
- **satisfeito**
- **aumentar**
- **sustentável**
- **multicultural**

The excerpts illustrate this clearly. In f6_pos_001, João Monteiro discusses “soft skills em projetos internacionais”, including resilience, flexibility and leadership. The same excerpt frames global learning as collaboration across borders to solve complex problems and promote collective well-being. In f6_pos_004, the opinion article explicitly states that PCIs help students develop “habilidades digitais, linguísticas, interculturais e de trabalho em equipe” and that cultural exchange promotes communication, tolerance, cooperation and global collaboration. In f6_pos_006, the benefits of COIL are listed as intercultural collaboration, linguistic development, digital competence and socioemotional learning.

Thus, this pole includes an evaluative and promotional discourse: PCIs/COIL are represented as beneficial, transformative, inclusive and professionally valuable.

### 4. Methodological and conceptual framing of COIL/Virtual Exchange

The positive pole also includes a conceptual-academic register. Many loading words point to definition, theorisation and methodological framing:

- **definição**
- **teoria**
- **método**
- **nomenclatura**
- **currículo**
- **disciplina**
- **eixo**
- **transversal**
- **temático**
- **interdisciplinar**
- **investigação**
- **articular**
- **questionamento**
- **especialista**
- **histórico**

The examples show speakers defining COIL, Virtual Exchange, Internationalisation at Home and Internationalisation of the Curriculum. In f6_pos_005, Luciane Stallivieri presents definitions of internationalisation, COIL and Virtual Exchange, and argues that internationalisation should be a transversal axis articulating teaching, research and extension. In f6_pos_001, Adam Freed provides a historical account of COIL and discusses definitions, publications, theory and practice. In f6_pos_006, speakers bring definitions of Virtual Exchange and COIL and summarise phases of COIL projects.

This means the factor pole is not only about practical event reporting; it also captures a discourse in which the newsletter **educates readers about concepts, frameworks and methodologies of international virtual collaboration**.

### 5. Applied, discipline-specific project discourse

Some of the strongest loading words initially appear less obviously connected to internationalisation, such as:

- **torque**
- **homologação**
- **norma**
- **veículo**
- **veicular**
- **emissão**
- **poluente**
- **cana-de-açúcar**
- **suprimento**
- **logística**
- **empresarial**
- **café**
- **consumo**
- **ambiental**

The examples clarify their role. These are not random technical terms; they occur in reports of **discipline-specific PCI/COIL projects**. In f6_pos_005, for example, the Jornada dos PCIs includes presentations about vehicle inspection and homologation with Chilean partners, logistics and sustainability with Colombian partners, agricultural products such as coffee and sugarcane, environmental impact, supply chains and business logistics.

These terms suggest that the pole captures a discourse of **international virtual exchange applied across technical, professional and disciplinary domains**. The PCIs are represented not only as language or culture projects, but as embedded in technology, logistics, business, agriculture, sustainability, vehicle engineering and applied research.

This applied-project strand is important because it shows how the pole combines **internationalisation discourse** with the practical identity of Fatecs and Centro Paula Souza: technology, professional education, extension, applied research and employability.

## Editions driving the pole

The pole is most clearly driven by:

### **vem_ed_29**
This edition has the highest mean score and contains the very high-scoring text f6_pos_001. Its discourse centres on:

- IVES Cesu 2025
- COIL and Virtual Exchange
- international speakers
- soft skills
- global learning
- interculturality
- internationalisation of the curriculum
- Faubai 2025
- AI and COIL
- cultural exchange in PCIs

The edition appears to be thematically focused on **international virtual exchange as global learning and curriculum internationalisation**.

### **vem_ed_32**
This edition has the second-highest mean score and contains the very high-scoring text f6_pos_005. Its discourse centres on:

- Jornada dos PCIs 2025
- definitions and histories of PCIs/COIL
- statistics and institutional growth
- presentations of PCI projects
- IVEC 2025
- COIL workshops
- visiting international academics
- UNICollaboration webinar
- benefits of COIL for students, teachers and institutions

This edition seems to intensify the institutional/event-reporting aspect of the pole, presenting PCIs as a mature, expanding and internationally recognised programme.

Other editions with moderately positive means, such as **vem_ed_03**, **vem_ed_12**, **vem_ed_15**, **vem_ed_28**, **vem_ed_20** and **vem_ed_10**, may contain related discourse, but the available examples indicate that the pole is especially shaped by editions 29 and 32.

## Possible labels for f6 positive

### Preferred label  
**“Institutionalised Virtual Exchange and COIL Internationalisation”**

This label captures the strongest and most recurrent elements: PCIs/COIL/Virtual Exchange, higher-education internationalisation, institutional organisation, events, definitions, project reports and pedagogical benefits.

### Alternative labels

1. **“COIL/PCI Event Reporting and Global Learning Discourse”**  
   Emphasises the genre-like quality of the pole: symposiums, jornadas, webinars, conferences, presentations, recordings and summaries, combined with global-learning terminology.

2. **“Virtual Internationalisation, Soft Skills and Intercultural Collaboration”**  
   Highlights the pedagogical and evaluative strand: soft skills, socioemotional competence, empathy, resilience, multicultural collaboration and student transformation.

3. **“Applied Internationalisation through Collaborative Online Projects”**  
   Captures the fact that many texts report discipline-specific PCI projects in logistics, sustainability, vehicles, business, languages, agriculture and technology.

4. **“Institutional Discourse of PCIs as Teaching, Research and Extension”**  
   Suitable if the emphasis is on how VEm presents PCIs as linking teaching, research, extension and internationalisation within Centro Paula Souza/Fatecs.

---

## Summary interpretation

The positive pole of Factor 6 represents a discourse of **organised, institutionally supported virtual internationalisation in higher education**, centred on PCIs/COIL/Virtual Exchange. It combines event reporting, conceptual explanation, project dissemination and positive evaluation of pedagogical outcomes. The pole is driven especially by VEm editions 29 and 32, where the newsletter reports major internationalisation events such as IVES Cesu 2025, Faubai 2025, Jornada dos PCIs 2025, IVEC 2025 and UNICollaboration activities. Its lexical profile combines institutional/event vocabulary, COIL and internationalisation terminology, soft-skills and intercultural-benefit language, and applied technical vocabulary from discipline-specific collaborative projects.
